Business problem & methodology

Operations teams need a consolidated, record-level view of CRM data defects before they can prioritize remediation. This demonstration evaluates a fictional contact extract and produces traceable validation exceptions.

  • Scope: active contacts only. Missing registration applies to advisors only.
  • Duplicate checks: normalized, nonblank email or registration ID shared by multiple active contacts; evaluated globally before filters so cross-region duplicates remain flagged.
  • Segment mismatch: both segment fields are populated and differ after trimming and case normalization.
  • Incomplete contact: missing name or email. Records may fail multiple rules.
  • Pass rate = (active contacts − distinct affected contacts) / active contacts. No data returns no rate.

Suggested actions: route duplicate identifiers to data stewards, assign missing-field exceptions to record owners, and track remediation through repeat refreshes. No remediation or business outcome is claimed for this demo.
